How to ask for a raise, written for you

Knowing how to ask for a raise is half the battle, and the blank email is the other half. The Zodumb Ask for a Raise Generator writes the request for you. Pick who you are asking, your main reason and a tone, and get a raise request email, a quick Slack message to set up a meeting, or talking points to say out loud.

Serious drafts or silly ones

Three tones are meant for real use: Confident (a clear ask with no apologizing), Polite (warm and appreciative, still direct) and Data-driven (leads with your contributions and market context). Two are just for fun: Dramatic (a movie speech about your worth) and Absurd (reasoning that gets sillier as it goes). Every draft gets an "odds of a yes" score. It is a joke, not a prediction.

Who are you asking, and why?

Choose your boss, manager, HR, the founder or CEO, or a client if you freelance and want to raise your rates. Then pick your main reason: more responsibility, strong results, pay below the market rate, no raise in a while, a competing offer, or the cost of living. Add real details, like how long you have been in the role or a project you led, and the draft uses them. It never invents numbers or achievements you did not give it.

Example raise request messages

  • Confident, email to manager: "Hi Sam, I'd like to set up time this week to discuss my compensation. Over the past year I've taken on the client onboarding work on top of my original role, and I'd like my pay to reflect that. Would Thursday or Friday work?"
  • Polite, Slack to boss: "Hey! Could we grab 20 minutes sometime next week? I'd love to talk about my role and compensation. No rush on timing, just want to get it on the calendar."
  • Data-driven, talking points: "I've been in this role for two years. In that time my responsibilities have grown to include managing the vendor budget. Based on what similar roles pay, I'd like to discuss bringing my salary in line with the market."
  • Absurd, email to manager: "I have calculated that I now do the work of 1.4 employees, and the 0.4 is getting tired. I propose a raise, or at minimum a second chair."

Frequently asked questions

How do I ask for a raise?

Ask for a meeting, explain what you have contributed and how your role has grown, and make a clear request. Pick a serious tone in the generator, add a few real details, and it drafts the email or talking points for you.

Should I ask for a raise by email or in person?

Many people send a short email or Slack message to set up a meeting, then make the case in person. The generator can write either: an email, a Slack message, or talking points to say out loud.

What should I say when asking for a raise?

Focus on your contributions, any added responsibility, and how your pay compares to the market for your role. Keep it positive and specific, and ask for a conversation rather than issuing an ultimatum.

Can I use it to raise my freelance rates?

Yes. Pick Client as the person you are asking, and the generator writes a rate increase message instead of a salary request.

Will it make up numbers or achievements?

No. It only uses the details you type in. If you leave them out, the draft stays general so you can add your own specifics before sending.
