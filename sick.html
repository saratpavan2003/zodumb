What is the Zodumb Call In Sick Generator?

The Call In Sick Generator is a free, AI-powered sick day excuse generator. Pick who you're telling, how believable you want the excuse to sound, and how you're sending it — then get an instant, ready-to-send message. No blank-page stress, no over-explaining, no typing it yourself.

Who can I send an excuse to?

Choose from boss, manager, HR, teacher, professor, coach, client, or the group chat. Each recipient nudges the tone — a message to HR reads differently than one to your roommate group chat.

Pick your believability

Every excuse is generated at a chosen vibe: Believable (genuinely plausible), Dramatic (soap-opera stakes), Vague (zero specifics, maximum mystery), Unhinged (chaotic honesty), or Emergency (an escalating fake crisis). It's a comedy tool — the believability score is a punchline, not a promise.

Text, Slack, or email — your choice

Select the format and the generator writes accordingly: a short casual text message, a quick Slack message, or a full email complete with a subject line and sign-off.

How it works

  1. Pick who you're messaging and how believable you want to sound.
  2. Choose text, Slack, or email as the format.
  3. Optionally add a hint (e.g. "food poisoning" or "the dog").
  4. Get an instant, ready-to-copy excuse — plus a backup line in case anyone asks questions.
  5. Copy it as text or download a shareable image card.
