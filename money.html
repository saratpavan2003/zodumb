Why I can't lend you money — a generator for saying no

Someone asked to borrow money and you don't want to lend it. This free, AI-powered excuse generator writes the "no" for you. Pick who's asking, how gentle (or absurd) you want to be, and how you're sending it, then get an instant message ready to send.

Who's asking?

Choose a friend, best friend, sibling, parent, cousin, aunt or uncle, ex, coworker, or roommate. Saying no to your mom reads differently than saying no to your ex.

From gentle to absurd

Every message is written in the vibe you pick: Gentle (kind and relationship-saving), Honest (a clear, respectful no), Vague ("money's tight right now"), Dramatic (an over-the-top money tragedy), or Absurd (a ridiculous reason that escalates as it goes). Each one comes with a friendship survival score — a punchline, not a promise.

Text, email, or say it out loud

Get a short text message, a full email with a subject line, or a short script to say in person or on a call. You can also add how much they asked for and what it's for, and the message will work it in.

How it works

  1. Pick who's asking to borrow money.
  2. Pick how you want to say no: gentle, honest, vague, dramatic, or absurd.
  3. Optionally add the amount and what it's for.
  4. Get a ready-to-copy refusal, plus a backup line in case they ask again.
  5. Copy it as text or download a shareable image card.

Frequently asked questions

Is this financial advice?

No. This is a comedy tool. The messages are written by AI for fun. Whether to lend money is your call.

Will the message ever say yes?

No. Every message is a clear no, so you won't accidentally promise "next time".

What is the friendship survival score?

A joke rating of how likely your relationship survives the message.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Completely free, no sign-up. Pick your options and get your message instantly.
