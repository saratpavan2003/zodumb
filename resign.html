How to write a resignation letter that keeps the bridge standing

A good resignation letter is short and clear. It says you are resigning, gives your last day, thanks your employer, and offers to help with the handover. That is exactly what the three serious tones do: Professional (clean and neutral), Grateful (warm thanks, leaving on great terms) and Brief (as short as it can be while still polite). The serious letters never say anything negative about your employer or coworkers.

Two weeks notice, one month, or effective today

Choose two weeks, one month, or effective today. The letter states your last day relative to today, so it never invents a calendar date you did not give it. Pick your reason, such as a new job, going back to school, moving, taking a break or starting your own business, or choose Rather not say to leave it out.

Just for fun: Dramatic and Absurd

Two tones are not meant to be sent: Dramatic (a farewell speech worthy of a final episode) and Absurd (a reason for leaving that gets sillier the more you read). Every result gets a "bridges intact" score. It is a punchline, not a promise.

Example resignation messages

  • Professional, letter to manager, two weeks: "Please accept this letter as formal notice of my resignation. My last day will be two weeks from today. I'm grateful for the opportunities I've had here, and I'm happy to help make the transition as smooth as possible."
  • Grateful, letter to boss, one month: "I'm writing to let you know I'll be leaving to go back to school, with my last day one month from today. Working with you has taught me more than I can say, and I'll make sure everything is handed over properly."
  • Brief, Slack to team: "Some news: I've accepted a new role, and my last day is in two weeks. It's been great working with you all. I'll make sure my projects are in good hands before I go."
  • Absurd, letter to manager: "After careful thought, I am resigning to pursue my lifelong dream of finding out what the office plant does on weekends. My last day is in two weeks. Please water it."

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a resignation letter?

A good resignation letter says you are resigning, gives your last day, thanks your employer, and offers to help with the handover. Pick Professional, Grateful or Brief in the generator and it writes exactly that for you.

Do I have to give a reason for resigning?

No. Choose the Rather Not Say option and the letter will leave the reason out entirely.

What is a two weeks notice letter?

It is a resignation letter that gives your employer two weeks before your last day. Pick Two weeks as your notice and the letter says your last day is two weeks from today.

Many people tell their manager in person or on a call first, then send a formal letter by email. The generator can write the letter, a Slack message to your team, or talking points for the conversation.
